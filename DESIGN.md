## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a fresh, dependable, and deeply authentic agricultural identity tailored for Gujarat's agrarian trade. It connects multi-generational farmers directly with urban and suburban households, balancing the raw vitality of Gujarat’s fertile soil with the pristine utility of a modern digital commerce platform.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Modernism** with a tactile agrarian soul. It rejects artificial ultra-slick tech clichés in favor of sunlit organic warmth, rooted tones, and grounded structural forms. The experience evokes immediate confidence: crops harvested at dawn in Anand or Navsari, arriving fresh at a family table in Ahmedabad or Surat by afternoon.

Visual principles:
- **Agrarian Authenticity:** Subtle earthy textures, crisp field botanicals, and proud regional origins instead of generic clip-art produce.
- **Uncompromising Clarity:** High-contrast, scannable data for bilingual ease across English and Gujarati scripts, functional in harsh outdoor sunlight on mid-tier mobile displays.
- **Organic Assurance:** Soft contours, natural soil-and-leaf contrast, and certified farm badges that instill absolute trust in product safety, grading, and fair farmer compensation.

## Colors

The palette directly mirrors the cycle of Gujarati agriculture: lush monsoon foliage, rich alluvial plains, and the golden ochre of ripe grain harvests.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#247A45`):** The vibrant foliage green. Used for key call-to-actions, checkout triggers, stock availability indicators, and prominent branding elements.
- **Deep Canopy (`#174D32`):** Grounded evergreen. Applied to primary headings, high-level headers, and high-emphasis agricultural certification marks.
- **Secondary / Earth Ochre (`#E99A42`):** Sun-drenched saffron ochre. Used selectively for harvest timers, seasonal highlights, farm location markers, and premium grading chips.
- **Tertiary / Fresh Lime (`#A7D66D`):** Vibrant sprout green. Reserved for success alerts, freshly harvested tags, and micro-interaction accents.

### Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Background (`#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas offering maximum contrast.
- **Surface Light Green (`#F0F7EC`):** Soft meadow tint for category backgrounds, farm profile sidebars, and nested item groups.
- **Warm Cream (`#FFF9EF`):** Hearth tone for farmer story modules, regional recipe showcases, and heirloom produce cards.
- **Border / Outline (`#E2ECE5`):** Subtle vegetal grey for crisp, non-distracting borders.
- **Text Dark (`#26352B`):** Deep charcoal-moss for body and headline typography, preserving readability without the harshness of pure black.
- **Text Muted (`#5A6E61`):** Subdued moss grey for secondary metadata, weight units, and district labels.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes bilingual legibility, robust optical hierarchy, and immediate scannability across outdoor environments.

- **Headlines:** Set in `Epilogue` (SemiBold / Bold), offering a balanced, confident contemporary geometry with organic rhythm, echoing the warmth of hand-painted APMC market signage while remaining rigorously modern.
- **Body & Data:** Set in `Inter`, chosen for its pristine screen legibility, expansive Unicode support, tabular numerals for market pricing (`₹/kg`), and neutral utility.
- **Bilingual & Script Harmonization:** When pairing Latin copy with Gujarati text (`Noto Sans Gujarati`), line heights expand by +4px to ensure zero clipping of upper and lower vowel matras (*kano, matra, rasva, dirgha*). Heading-to-body pairings maintain strict optical size alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8pt progressive grid** operating within a responsive fluid architecture.

### Grid Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 fluid columns, `margin: 1rem` (16px), `gutter: 1rem` (16px). Full-width produce cards, horizontal carousel scrollers for district filters, bottom-pinned purchase bars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 fluid columns, `margin: 2rem` (32px), `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Dual-column listing views with persistent cart access.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 fluid columns constrained to a max canvas width of `1280px`, `margin: 3rem` (48px), `gutter: 2rem` (32px). Dedicated left rail for farm filters (taluka, certification, harvest date) and a right primary product canvas.

### Rhythmic Rules
- **Component Padding:** Standard card interior padding is pinned to `space-lg` (24px) on desktop and `space-md` (16px) on mobile.
- **Micro-Gaps:** Badges and metadata chips lock strictly to `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) gaps to preserve tight semantic grouping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layering** paired with **soft agrarian shadows**. Flat, high-glare interfaces alienate users in bright sunlight; heavy artificial drop-shadows look synthetic. The system applies organic, diffuse shadows tinted with deep vegetal green (`#174D32`).

### Depth Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` or meadow `#F0F7EC`. Zero shadow.
- **Tier 1 (Cards, Produce Cells, District Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a crisp structural hairline border (`1px solid #E2ECE5`) and an ambient, sunlit drop: `0px 2px 8px -1px rgba(23, 77, 50, 0.05)`.
- **Tier 2 (Sticky Headers, Harvest Filters, Active Dropdowns):** Subtle ambient lift: `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(23, 77, 50, 0.08)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Farmer Traceability Drawers, Mobile Bottom Sheets):** High elevation layer: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(23, 77, 50, 0.14)`. Backdrop is covered with an organic veil of `#174D32` at 40% opacity.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **natural, organic curvature** (`roundedness: 2`) that conveys freshness and accessibility without entering toy-like territory.

- **Primary Produce & Farmer Cards:** Use `rounded-lg` (16px) on desktop and `rounded` (12px) on mobile, framing photographs of farm lots and fresh harvests cleanly.
- **Interactive Buttons & Input Fields:** Standardized at `rounded` (8px) to retain structural authority and tap accuracy.
- **Badges, District Tags & Metric Counters:** Set to fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish informational status markers from actionable rectangular cards.
- **Product Image Containers:** Bound within `rounded` (12px) masks with subtle internal inset lines to prevent image blowouts on high-brightness displays.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Buy Direct):** Deep foliage green (`#247A45`) fill with pure white label, `8px` corner radius, `48px` minimum height for comfortable outdoor thumb taps. Hover state darkens to `#174D32`.
- **Secondary Action (View Farm Profile):** Outlined with `1.5px solid #247A45`, transparent fill, `#247A45` text. On interaction, transitions to surface `#F0F7EC`.
- **Harvest Accent (Seasonal Pre-order):** Saffron ochre (`#E99A42`) fill with dark moss (`#26352B`) text, signaling urgent harvest-bound crops.

### District & Region Chips
- Pill-shaped tags designating production regions (e.g., *Surat*, *Anand*, *Navsari*, *Rajkot*, *Junagadh*).
- **Default State:** `#F0F7EC` background, `#247A45` text, `1px solid #E2ECE5`.
- **Selected State:** `#247A45` background with pure white text and an integrated pin icon. Includes optional Gujarati subtitle tag (*e.g., આણંદ / Anand*).

### Produce & Market Cards
- Encased in `1px solid #E2ECE5` over pure `#FFFFFF`, featuring a `16px` border radius.
- Contains a 1:1 or 4:3 product visual, floating freshness badge on the top-left, farmer identification line, price per unit with bold Gujarati & Indian Rupee glyph (`₹`), and an instant stepper counter (`-` 1 `+`).

### Agricultural & Freshness Badges
- **Harvest Time Badge:** Warm Cream (`#FFF9EF`) background with `#E99A42` text: "Harvested: Today 6:00 AM".
- **Certification Pill:** Meadow green (`#F0F7EC`) background with `#247A45` text and leaf glyph: "Prakrutik Kheti (Organic)".

### Form Inputs & Search
- Generous `48px` height, `8px` radius, styled with `#FFFFFF` fill, `#26352B` text, and `#E2ECE5` border.
- **Focus State:** 2px ring in primary green (`#247A45`) with zero color distortion. Search bars feature voice-input micro-triggers for rural accessibility.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- `20px` touch targets with `4px` radius for checkboxes and round profile for radios. Active state fills with `#247A45` displaying a crisp white indicator tick.

### Farmer Profile & Traceability Drawer
- Surfaces using `#FFF9EF` or `#F0F7EC` with dedicated farmer photo, taluka/district verification badge, soil health data, and real-time mandi benchmark rates.
